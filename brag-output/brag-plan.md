## Answers

- **What is it?** A Brazilian industrial engineering firm (Campinas/SP, 22 years). It designs and runs plant projects from concept to start-up.
- **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?** Plants in pulp & paper, food, beverages, pharma, cosmetics, chemicals, fertilizers and animal nutrition. It handles expansions (brownfield) and new plants (greenfield), and supplies outsourced engineering labour.
- **What sets it apart?** It covers every phase of a project. Its 3D laser scanning turns an existing plant into a point cloud and then a precise 3D model.
- **Core message:** Belsys sells engineering *projects* (not equipment): "especializada em Projetos de Engenharia Industrial". Also "Precisão milimétrica", and a client list with Klabin, Suzano, Unilever, Goodyear, Tetra Pak and Honeywell.
- **Visual hook:** a field of points (the brand's network motif, and a nod to the point cloud) pulling together into the logo.
- **Real UI / flow to show:** field laser scan → point cloud → 3D model, using the real photos and renders from p.13. Also the real 3D models from the cases pages and the real client logo wall from p.23.
- **One-line caption:** "Projetos de engenharia para a indústria brasileira: 22 anos, do conceitual à implantação."
